Our Story

A Royal Legacy Resurrected

LalQila is not just a restaurant; it is a journey back in time. Inspired by the majestic Red Fort, we bring the opulent lifestyle of the Mughal Emperors to the modern era.

Our thematic setting features intricate architecture, courtyards, and a mesmerizing ambiance. Relish our legendary buffet spread encompassing traditional Pakistani, Mughlai, Barbecue, and Continental cuisines, prepared by master chefs using centuries-old secret recipes.
